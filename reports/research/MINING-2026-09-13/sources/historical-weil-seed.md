# Weil–Dagger QFT and the RH Positivity Target

## Objective

Construct an independently defined reflection-positive or operator-algebraic system whose two-point form equals the Weil explicit-formula quadratic form.

The target implication is:

```text
independent positive state + exact state-preserving comparison to the Weil test algebra => RH
```

The project does not begin by placing zeta zeros in an operator spectrum.

## Existing theorem seed

Use the Weil pre-Krein/GNS, tautology detector, prime-gas no-go, Deninger–Hodge sufficiency, and dagger-transfer sections of `platonic_reconstruction.tex`.

## Work packages

1. Fix the exact Weil test-function algebra, involution, topology, and sign convention.
2. Construct the unconditional pre-Krein representation and convolution action.
3. Survey candidate independent positive systems: Connes/Meyer/Deninger/transfer-operator/foliated-flow/QFT models.
4. For one candidate, construct a concrete observable map from test functions.
5. Prove the explicit-formula state identity without assuming zero placement.
6. Prove reflection positivity or a positive GNS state independently.
7. Establish operator domains and determinant regularization if a Hilbert–Pólya operator is claimed.

## Failure tests

Reject any model when:

- the zeta zeros are inserted as eigenvalues;
- positivity is equivalent to RH by definition and no independent positive mechanism is given;
- a Bost–Connes `log n` spectrum is relabeled as the zero spectrum;
- a functional equation is used as positivity;
- a complex factorization equivalence lacks a dagger/state enhancement.

## Minimum publishable result

A complete no-go/recognition theorem for a broad class of candidate QFT constructions, plus one nontrivial positive subclass of Weil test functions derived from an independent state.
