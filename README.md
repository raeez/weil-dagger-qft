# The Zeta–Weil Form and Nonunital GNS Transfer

Raeez Lorgat

The full zeta--Weil functional is written on a concrete nonunital convolution $*$-algebra, with its polar, archimedean, and prime-power terms fixed. Weil's theorem identifies positivity on convolution squares with the Riemann hypothesis. We prove the algebraic GNS theorem in the nonunital setting, including automatic closability and the exact bounded multiplier criterion, and show that dagger transfer extends isometrically to Hilbert completions. A Gaussian calculation proves that the Weil functional has no positive unitization of finite mass, even if RH holds. Thus a positive dagger realization of the Weil functional would prove the Riemann hypothesis only when its positivity is obtained independently of the zeros or of Weil positivity itself. Its target must retain a nonunital functional domain or an infinite weight. Constructing this arithmetic realization remains open.
