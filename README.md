# The Zeta–Weil Form and Nonunital GNS Transfer

This eight-page paper fixes the Fourier, involution, measure, polar,
archimedean, and prime-power conventions for the full zeta--Weil functional
on a concrete nonunital convolution $*$-algebra.  Its arithmetic statement is
Weil's exact criterion
\[
  \mathrm{RH}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  L_\zeta(f^*\star f)\geq 0\qquad\text{for every }f\in\mathcal A_\zeta.
\]
Thus Weil positivity is equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis; it is not an
independent proof of it.

The paper also proves the nonunital algebraic GNS construction, automatic
closability of its common-domain multipliers, and the exact condition for a
multiplier to extend boundedly.  A $*$-homomorphism pulling back a positive
functional induces an isometric map of algebraic GNS spaces and a unique
isometry of their Hilbert completions, with bounded intertwining under
$C^*$-hypotheses.  For nonunital $C^*$-algebras it gives the sharp positive
unitization criterion and identifies the cyclic vector as the limit of every
positive contractive two-sided approximate identity for the minimal
unitization.

Explicit examples delimit these conclusions: a symmetric closable operator
need not have a self-adjoint extension, algebraic positive unitization can
fail, the isotropic cone of an indefinite form is not a quotient subspace,
and a radical quotient alone does not produce a Krein space or positivity.
Accordingly, no theorem passing from bare pre-Krein data to a positive GNS
representation is asserted.
The paper ends with the open problem of constructing an independent positive
dagger realization of $L_\zeta$ from its arithmetic local terms, without
using RH, the zeta zeros, or the equivalent Weil positivity.
