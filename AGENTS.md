# Agent Rules

## Golden rule: no project management at any scale whatsoever in the manuscript

Reader-facing prose is exclusively mathematical or physical, at every scale
from title and metadata to proofs and footnotes. Never mention agents, prompts,
critiques, audits, workflows, tasks, commits, tests, progress, ownership, or
revision history. Distinguish unconditional, conditional, conjectural, and
open assertions exactly. The archive supplies untrusted questions only.

Any positivity mechanism must be independent of the desired zero placement;
do not encode the conclusion in the spectrum or state. Use the structural
virtues of Witten's major 1980s papers without imitating wording. Pass these
rules to every subagent.
