\begin{abstract}
The explicit formula supplies a Hermitian form, but its positivity cannot be
deduced from a functional equation alone.  We isolate a sufficient recognition
datum: an independently positive state and a state-preserving map from the
test-function algebra.  The resulting positivity transfer is formal; the
construction of such a state without presupposing zero placement is the open
mathematical problem.
\end{abstract}

\section{The recognition datum}

Let $\mathcal A$ be a complex $*$-algebra and let
$W:\mathcal A\to\mathbb C$ be a linear functional, Hermitian in the sense that
$W(a^*)=\overline{W(a)}$.  Write
\[
 q_W(a)=W(a^*a).
\]
Let $(\mathcal B,\omega)$ be another $*$-algebra equipped with a positive
state, so $\omega(b^*b)\geq0$.

\begin{theorem}[Dagger transfer]
If $T:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ is a $*$-homomorphism and
$W=\omega\circ T$, then $q_W(a)\geq0$ for every $a\in\mathcal A$.
Moreover the null space of $q_W$ is the inverse image under $T$ of the GNS
null space of $\omega$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $a\in\mathcal A$,
\[
 q_W(a)=\omega(T(a^*a))=\omega(T(a)^*T(a))\geq0.
\]
The same identity shows that $q_W(a)=0$ exactly when $T(a)$ represents zero
in the GNS seminorm.
\end{proof}

When $W$ is a Weil explicit-formula functional on a precisely stated test
algebra, this theorem transfers positivity from $\omega$.  It does not produce
$\mathcal B$, $\omega$, or $T$, and it has content only if their construction
does not assume the desired location of the zeros.

\begin{problem}
Construct an operator-algebraic or reflection-positive field-theoretic triple
$(\mathcal B,\omega,T)$ for the full Weil test class, prove the exact state
identity with all archimedean and prime terms, and establish positivity without
using an equivalent zero-placement assertion.
\end{problem}
